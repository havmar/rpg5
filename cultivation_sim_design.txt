================================================================================
CULTIVATION WORLD SIMULATOR — DESIGN DOCUMENT
A wuxia/xianxia career simulation with an AI DM as narrative renderer
================================================================================

CORE PREMISE
------------
The game is a simulation kernel with a narrative lens on top. The world runs as
a mechanical process (Python scripts); the AI DM's job is to read the logs and
tell you what they meant. Design order: SIM FIRST, CHEAT LATER. No narrative
gravity, no plot armor, no dramaturgy in version one. If the raw event logs of
individual agents already read like lives, the kernel is right, and any later
AI narration is a renderer over honest data rather than fiction papering over
noise.

The base deliverable is not a game but a world generator: take a starting
class of ~64 students across several sects and simulate their careers over
centuries until a few reach the top. The generations above (elders) and below
(future intakes) are simulated by the same rules. A player character is added
later as "agent 65" — same rules, same funnel — with a richer, game-like
resource layer unfolded only for them.

An elegant loop follows from this: run the simulation for 200 years first,
and the survivors ARE the elders, rivals, and legends of the world the player
is born into. Every campaign world is the fossil record of a previous
simulation.


================================================================================
PART I — THE GENRE FRAME (what the mechanics must reproduce)
================================================================================

1. TWO AXES INSTEAD OF ONE POWER STAT
   - REALM: the visible power tier (Qi Condensation -> Foundation
     Establishment -> Core Formation -> Nascent Soul -> ...). Defines what a
     character can do.
   - DAO HEART / INSIGHT: self-mastery, conviction, understanding of one's
     personal path. Determines whether a character CAN advance and survive
     advancing. Realm is loud; insight is the actual engine of careers.

2. TIERED POWER, EMBRACED RATHER THAN BALANCED
   Cultivation fiction is explicitly unbalanced, and the design encodes it:
   - Same realm: genuine contest.
   - One realm apart: the weaker side can only survive, flee, delay — unless
     they bring narrative assets (treasures, formations, ambush, allies,
     sacrifice, a hidden trump card).
   - Two realms apart: not a fight at all.
   This "tyranny of realms" makes power gaps feel real and makes acquiring
   trump cards and allies a central strategic activity — exactly how the
   genre's protagonists operate.

3. BREAKTHROUGH AS EVENT, NOT ACCUMULATION
   Advancement requires three distinct things:
   - QI: grindable (time, spirit stones, pills, good environments).
   - INSIGHT: non-grindable; earned only through lived adversity — defeats,
     near-death, betrayal, loss, epiphany, acts true to one's dao.
   - FOUNDATION QUALITY: how cleanly previous stages were built; shortcuts
     (pill-stacking) give power now and poison the ceiling later.
   The breakthrough itself is a dramatic set-piece (tribulation) with real
   failure states: qi deviation, heart demons taking root, crippled
   cultivation, death.

4. THE DAO AND ITS SHADOW
   Each character has a personal dao — a principle, not just an element
   ("the sword that never lies", "the dao of endurance"). Acting in accord
   with it generates insight; betraying it plants HEART DEMONS that stay
   dormant and ambush the character at the next tribulation. Personality
   flaws are literally the progression bottleneck.

5. TIME AND LIFESPAN AS THE DEEP ECONOMY
   Careers span 50–300 years. Each realm extends lifespan, creating a slow
   clock: stall too long and you die of old age before the next breakthrough.
   The constant tension: seclusion (qi, safety, but time burns and the world
   moves on) versus the world (insight, resources, grudges, danger).
   Time-skips are first-class; rivals also cultivate offscreen.

6. THE SOCIAL LEDGER
   Face, favors, karma, sect standing, oaths, sworn brotherhood, life-debts.
   Grudges are tracked as living entities: humiliate a young master today
   and his Nascent Soul grandfather is a problem in twenty years. Killing
   solves problems but generates karma; sparing enemies generates future
   threats. Often more consequential than combat.

7. SUPPORTING SUBSYSTEMS (later, mostly player-facing)
   - Techniques as collectible, comprehension-leveled knowledge; incomplete
     or flawed manuals as risk/reward traps.
   - Pills with toxicity buildup and diminishing returns.
   - "Fortuitous encounters" as a luck currency the game spends on agents.
   - Scale zoom per realm tier: village stakes -> sect politics -> empires
     -> the heavens.

   The unifying idea: the system simulates not "getting stronger" but THE
   PRICE of getting stronger — time, attachments, integrity, and the people
   left behind or made into enemies.


================================================================================
PART II — SIMULATION ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================

1. UNIFORM AGENT MODEL (the chosen approach)
   Everyone — elders, the focal cohort, juniors — runs identical rules at
   full(ish) fidelity. Generations emerge from recruitment and death, not
   from special code. Only the eventual PLAYER gets a deluxe layer (real
   inventory, techniques, scene-level play); NPCs compress all of that into
   a single resource score. Population equilibrium of ~150–300 agents at one
   action and a handful of events per agent-year is computationally trivial.

   (A three-band fidelity system — full sim for the cohort, institutional
   sim for elders, statistical pools below — was considered and set aside;
   it becomes relevant only if the world scales far beyond a few hundred
   agents. Keep the idea in reserve: promotion/demotion between bands, where
   an irrelevant agent degrades to coarse simulation and an exceptional
   statistical youngster gets instantiated as a full agent.)

2. TICK STRUCTURE
   Base clock: one year (careers span centuries; finer ticks drown in
   noise). Run 200–300 years per world. Discrete EVENTS layered over the
   ticks (tournaments, expeditions, feuds, successions) are where
   trajectories diverge; the years between are accumulation and drift.

3. THE CAREER ENGINE
   Careers emerge from the interaction of fixed endowment (talent), evolving
   personality (traits), relationships, and streaky luck. Because insight
   comes only from adversity actually experienced, the sim naturally
   reproduces the genre's core irony with no scripting: the sheltered genius
   who plateaus at their first real tribulation; the battered underdog who
   breaks through.

4. ATTRITION AS THE SHAPE OF THE STORY
   Target funnel per intake of 64, over a century-plus:
     ~35–45 reach realm 2, ~15 reach realm 3, ~4–6 reach realm 4,
     0–2 ever touch the top.
   Deaths need textured causes: tribulations, expeditions, feuds, qi
   deviation, war, old age. Crucially, include VOLUNTARY EXITS — students
   who marry out, take administrative posts, lose conviction. A funnel that
   only kills feels like a meat grinder; one where people also choose
   ordinary lives feels like a world.

5. THE LAYER ABOVE AS WEATHER
   Elders generate the environment the cohort climbs through: which sect is
   ascendant, which resources are contested, wars, power vacuums. They are
   also the opportunity-injection engine: a dying elder seeks an heir, war
   creates battlefield promotions, a purge orphans talented students. The
   top of the world needs TEMPO — shifts every 10–30 years — so the cohort
   never climbs a static ladder.

6. RANDOMNESS WITH MEMORY (post-toy refinements)
   Pure dice produce noise-shaped careers. Two correctives:
   - DESTINY HOOKS: each agent rolls a small number at creation (hidden
     bloodline, doomed love, an old family enemy); the event system
     preferentially fires on them. Arc-shaped lives without predetermined
     outcomes.
   - STREAKY LUCK: fortune compounds in this genre (treasures attract
     treasures, attention attracts danger); let recent outcomes bias event
     selection slightly.

7. EVENT LOGGING AS THE NARRATIVE SUBSTRATE
   Every consequential outcome writes a structured entry: year, actors,
   event, outcome, relationship deltas, WITNESSES. Log rumors as well as
   facts — cultivation drama runs on who knows what, and a rumor layer lets
   the DM generate misunderstandings, hidden identities, and reveals
   honestly from the data. The simulation says:
     "Y3 autumn: Student 17 defeated Student 41 in the tournament final;
      41's backer lost face; 41 gained grudge(17); 17 gained reputation +2
      and the attention of Elder Wu."
   The AI DM turns that into the scene.


================================================================================
PART III — THE TOY VERSION (first buildable artifact, ~300–500 lines)
================================================================================

One uniform agent model, one loop, three generations alive at once.

1. AGENT SHEET (~a dozen fields)
   - age
   - realm (1–6) and qi progress within realm (0–100)
   - talent (1–10, fixed at birth): multiplier on qi gain, soft cap on realm
   - insight (banked from lived events; required to break through)
   - burden (one counter standing in for heart demons / unresolved baggage)
   - resources (ONE number for wealth/pills/treasures; the player version
     later explodes this into real inventory — NPCs never need it)
   - sect, and standing within it
   - traits: 2–3 from a small pool
   - relationships: sparse list (rival, friend, master, lover, grudge),
     each with an intensity
   - epithets: list of strings, starts empty

2. TRAITS — THE PERSONALITY ENGINE (three jobs)
   Pool of 12–16: Proud, Cautious, Reckless, Ruthless, Loyal, Vengeful,
   Scholarly, Charming, Stubborn, Ascetic, Greedy, Humble, Righteous, Cold.
   - JOB 1 — weight the yearly action choice (Reckless favors adventure,
     Ascetic favors seclusion, Charming favors socializing).
   - JOB 2 — modify event outcomes (Proud cannot decline a duel; Cautious
     gets a flee bonus; Ruthless kills defeated rivals instead of
     humiliating them — which changes whether the sim generates corpses or
     grudges).
   - JOB 3 — MUTATE UNDER PRESSURE (the single best tool for making
     characters legible over time): a Proud agent crushed in public may
     become Broken or Humble; a Loyal agent betrayed becomes Vengeful;
     surviving a near-death breakthrough can turn anyone Ascetic. The log
     then shows people CHANGING, not just numbers moving.

3. EPITHETS — THE CHEAP MEMORABILITY TRICK
   Any sufficiently dramatic event stamps a descriptive tag: One-Armed,
   Tribulation-Scarred, Oathbreaker, Slayer-of-Wei, Twice-Failed. Zero
   mechanical weight in the toy; enormous readability value. "Han Yu the
   One-Armed reached Core Formation" compresses fifteen simulated years
   into a name, at the cost of a string append.

4. THE YEAR LOOP (three phases; run 200–300 years)

   ACTION PHASE — every agent picks one action, via softmax over trait
   weights plus situational nudges (low qi + rich sect -> cultivate; active
   grudge + Vengeful -> seek the enemy):
   - Cultivate: qi gain x talent; safe.
   - Seclude: more qi, but socially absent — relationships decay, the world
     moves on.
   - Adventure: roll a risk table: death / injury / nothing / resources /
     insight / fateful encounter.
   - Socialize/Scheme: shift standing and relationships; plant or resolve
     grudges.
   - Teach: elders by inclination (not rule); creates master–disciple
     edges, boosts the junior's qi gain.

   EVENT PHASE — collective events:
   - Tournament every 4 years: brackets within realm bands; results move
     standing, forge rivalries, and grant insight to LOSERS WHO SURVIVE.
   - Secret-realm expedition on a random timer: high death, high reward;
     volunteers skew Reckless/Greedy.
   - Sect feud: ignites when accumulated cross-sect grudge intensity passes
     a threshold.
   - Succession crisis: when a sect head dies.

   RESOLUTION PHASE — breakthrough attempts, aging, lifespan checks.

5. CONTESTS — ONE FORMULA
   power = realm x 10 + qi/10 + talent (+ small trait modifiers)
   Keep the tyranny of realms even in the toy: a full realm of difference
   means the lower side can only flee or die. It is what makes the funnel
   and the fear real.

   INSIGHT is granted by adversity, not victory: surviving a loss, a
   near-death, a betrayal, witnessing a higher-realm battle, losing someone
   loved. This single rule reproduces sheltered-genius-plateaus /
   battered-underdog-ascends with no scripting.

6. BREAKTHROUGH AND DEATH
   - At qi 100, attempt breakthrough if insight meets the realm threshold;
     otherwise STALL. Stalling agents increasingly choose Adventure (hunger
     for insight) — a natural feedback loop.
   - Success chance = base + talent + surplus insight - burden.
   - Failure costs qi; from realm 3 up it risks injury or death.
   - Lifespan realm-keyed: mortal ~80 years, each realm adds ~40–60.
   - Death arrives via tribulation, adventure, feud, war, or the quiet
     clock — and the clock matters: a talent-3 Stubborn agent grinding at
     the realm-2 wall until age 130 is exactly the tragic side character
     the sim should produce for free.

7. GENERATIONS FOR FREE
   Every 8 years each sect recruits an intake of fresh agents, age ~14,
   with rolled talent and traits and a couple of pre-seeded relationships
   (siblings, childhood friends, a hometown grudge). The "starting class of
   64" is simply the intake you choose to watch; elders are previous
   intakes that survived; the coming generation arrives on schedule.

8. OUTPUT IS THE PRODUCT
   Every consequential resolution appends a structured log line: year,
   actors, event, outcome, deltas, witnesses. The test: does a raw grep of
   one agent's lines read like a life?

     Y12  Ping Mei (Proud, Reckless, T7) defeats Han Yu in tournament
          final; Han Yu gains grudge(Ping Mei)
     Y15  Secret realm: Ping Mei maimed [epithet: One-Eyed], +12 insight;
          trait Reckless -> Cautious
     Y19  Ping Mei breaks through to Foundation (age 33)
     Y31  Feud, Wei ambush: Ping Mei flees (Cautious); sworn brother
          Luo Gan dies; gains grudge(Wei), +8 insight
     Y44  Ping Mei fails Core tribulation (burden 6); crippled, retires
          to teach
     Y52  Ping Mei's disciple Xie Lan reaches Foundation at 19

   Two derived views:
   - OBITUARY at each death: name, epithets, cause, realm reached, who
     grieved, who celebrated.
   - FINAL ROSTER at year 250.

9. TUNING TARGETS FOR FIRST RUNS
   - FUNNEL SHAPE: per intake of 64, roughly 35–45 to realm 2, a handful
     to realm 4, zero-to-two touching the top over a century. Tune death
     rates and the insight economy until this holds.
   - TALENT VS LUCK: sort final realm by starting talent. Monotone ranking
     = luck too weak, every run predetermined. Uncorrelated = talent is
     decoration. Target: visible correlation with famous exceptions.
   - DISTINGUISHABILITY: pick five random agents, read their logs, check
     each can be described in one sentence. If two lives blur, the fix is
     almost always more trait mutation and more relationship-driven
     events — not more stats.


================================================================================
PART IV — THE PLAYER LAYER (later)
================================================================================

The player character is agent 65: same rules, same funnel, no plot armor.
What changes is resolution, not physics:
- The single NPC resource number unfolds into real inventory, spirit
  stones, pills (with toxicity), treasures, techniques (collectible,
  comprehension-leveled, flawed-manual traps).
- The yearly action unfolds into scene-level play run by the AI DM, which
  shifts from historian to narrator around the player.
- The dao becomes explicit: the player states a principle; play in accord
  generates insight, betrayal plants heart demons that surface at
  tribulations.
- Tribulations become set-piece scenes with real failure states.
- The social ledger becomes visible and interactive: face, oaths, favors,
  karma, sworn brotherhood, grudges with due dates.

Everything around the player keeps running the toy rules — which, by then,
will have been proven able to carry a world on their own.


================================================================================
PART V — BUILD ORDER AND OPEN QUESTIONS
================================================================================

BUILD ORDER
1. Toy sim (Part III). Validate against the three tuning targets by
   reading raw logs. No AI involved.
2. Add refinements: destiny hooks, streaky luck, rumor/witness layer,
   voluntary exits, elder tempo.
3. AI DM as renderer: chronicle generation, obituaries, "state of the
   world" digests from logs.
4. World-generation mode: run 200 years, freeze, use survivors as the
   living setting.
5. Player layer on top of a generated world.

OPEN QUESTIONS (deliberately deferred)
- Narrative gravity: pure simulation will sometimes kill every interesting
  agent and elevate a boring one. Later, does the event system get to
  quietly thumb the scales toward drama (protecting rivalries until they
  resolve, timing reveals), or is the appeal precisely that the world is
  indifferent? Decision: SIM FIRST, CHEAT LATER — revisit only after the
  honest kernel is proven.
- When (if ever) the uniform agent model needs fidelity bands as the world
  scales.
- How much of the dao/heart-demon system NPCs eventually get (currently
  compressed into the burden counter).

================================================================================
PART VI — THE POLITICS LAYER (moralistic politics: full spec)
================================================================================

What this layer adds: a world of polities — empires, kingdoms, khanates,
city-states, tribes, villages — whose LEADERS' characters determine the
quality of life of the common people and of low-level cultivators. Bad
leaders are extractive and cruel; good leaders are lucky and poor. Taking a
throne is a genuine exit from the cultivator's path. The commoners never
become agents; they are a field (prosperity per place) that leaders push
down and heroes push up, and the field reaches back into the sim at the
recruitment gate and on the road.

Design stance: the moral economy must be MECHANICAL, not scripted. Karma is
a number fed by deeds and coupled to the existing luck system; virtue is
the good-luck lane, vice is the fast lane for resources. No event ever
checks "is this the villain". The single deliberate exception (the camera
constraint, §8) is documented as such.

This layer slots into the build order after the toy sim is proven; it does
not depend on the AI DM or the player layer.


1. GEOGRAPHY — THE NINE LANDS
   The world is a 3x3 grid of lands. The center is THE MIDDLE PLAIN:
   structurally the pseudo-China of the genre — a centralized bureaucratic
   empire, the largest population, and the seat of all four sects — but
   culturally a melting pot: it has NO name pool of its own. Its natives
   roll a descent from the six existing homeland pools (weighted evenly),
   and the mixed-surname chance is doubled there. No new language pools are
   added anywhere, and no Chinese name pool exists; the pseudo-China is a
   structure, not an ethnicity.

   The 8 outer slots are filled from the six existing NAME_LANDS pools. At
   world generation the rng picks TWO pools to supply two lands each —
   sibling nations sharing a language. Each pool carries a fixed secondary
   land name in the data tables, used only if that pool doubles:

       Spice Isles     / Coral Strand    (Indonesian)
       Sky Steppe      / Wolf Steppe     (Mongolian)
       Thousand Lakes  / Birch Marches   (Finnish)
       Glacier Coast   / Ashen Fjords    (Icelandic)
       River Kingdoms  / Lotus Delta     (Sanskrit)
       Sunset Plateau  / Salt Wastes     (Persian)

   The 8 lands are shuffled onto the outer slots (seeded rng); sibling
   lands are placed adjacent when the shuffle allows (one retry pass, then
   accept whatever falls). Adjacency: edge-adjacent lands are strong
   neighbors (war, trade, refugees); corner contact is weak (rumors, rare
   war). The center touches everything.

   The static per-pool "weight" is retired. Recruitment reach is now
   geographic: recruits are drawn with weight 4 from the Middle Plain, 2
   from edge lands, 1 from corner lands (tuning knobs). The per-cohort
   DOMINANT_LAND_BOOST mechanic is unchanged. An agent's `homeland` becomes
   a reference to a real place (their home settlement), not just a string.

2. PLACES
   Within a land, geography is a nested tree — this is where the politics
   lives:

       Place: name, kind (land / region / city / town / village / sect),
              parent, children, land (root ref),
              prosperity (float 0-10), baseline (4-6, the land's temper)

   Counts stay small: the Middle Plain has 3 regions of 3-4 settlements
   plus a capital city and the four sect seats; each outer land has 2-3
   regions of 2-3 settlements plus a capital. Roughly 70-90 places total.
   A land's prosperity is the mean of its settlements; regions likewise.
   Left alone, prosperity drifts 0.2/year toward the land's baseline.

   Settlement names are drawn from the land's own name pools (a surname or
   given-name stem) plus a small kind-keyed English suffix table ("Ford",
   "Wells", "Market", "Hold", "Rest"...). Middle Plain settlements draw
   stems from any pool — the melting pot shows on the map.

   Prosperity displays as words, never numbers, in the chronicle:
   0-1 starving, 2-3 desperate, 4-5 modest, 6-7 comfortable,
   8-9 prosperous, 10 golden.

3. POLITIES AND VASSALAGE
       Polity: pid, name, type (empire / kingdom / khanate / jarldom /
               city / tribe / sect), seat (place), territory (places ruled
               directly), leader (aid), liege (pid or None), vassals,
               unrest (int, >= 0), army (int), edicts (list)

   Type follows culture: Mongolian-pool lands produce khanates and tribes,
   Icelandic-pool lands jarldoms, the center the empire, others kingdoms
   and city-states. At worldgen: one sovereign per land; the Middle Plain
   empire rules its regions directly and holds 1-2 vassal city-states;
   each outer sovereign has 0-2 vassals. Roughly 15-25 polities.

   DIRECT RULE: the leader's rule style (§5) applies to every place in the
   territory. VASSALAGE: the vassal's own leader sets local policy and pays
   a yearly tribute (leader resources flowing upward); each of the liege's
   active edicts propagates to each vassal with 50% chance (mandates).
   Villages and towns have no leader agent — they are ruled by whichever
   polity holds their region. Sects are polities of type "sect", OUTSIDE
   the vassalage tree (spiritual exemption; the empire does not tax them,
   and knows better than to try). Their interface with the secular world is
   the petition (§9).

4. RULERS
   Leaders are ordinary Agents — the uniform agent model holds. Worldgen
   rulers are sect-less mortals: realm 1 (a capital sovereign is
   occasionally realm 2 — a cultivator ancestor on the throne), adult
   ages, traits rolled from the full pool with a court skew (raised odds
   of Proud, Power-Hungry, Righteous). They age and die on the mortal
   clock, which produces successions.

   New agent fields: `ruling` (pid or None), `home` (place ref),
   `karma` (int, §7).

   RULING LOCKS CULTIVATION. A ruler's action phase is replaced by a
   single RULE action: no cultivate, no seclude, no adventure, no teach.
   Qi gain is zero; insight arrives only through governance adversity
   (a revolt survived, a war lost, a betrayal by a vassal). Their realm
   still counts fully in any contest — the tyranny of realms is exactly
   what keeps a Core Formation king on his throne for a century.

   Taking a throne is logged as a variant of the voluntary exit, but the
   agent stays fully simulated. Paths onto thrones: succession, usurpation,
   revolt championship, invitation (§9). Paths off: death, deposition, and
   ABDICATION — an Ascetic or Broken ruler, or one grown old and weary,
   may abdicate and resume cultivation with qi intact while the world has
   moved on. The deposed king returning to the mountain is a life the sim
   should produce for free.

   POWER CORRUPTS: each ruling year there is a small chance the ruler
   mutates one step down the ladder Greedy -> Power-Hungry -> Cruel,
   suppressed by Righteous/Humble/Loyal and raised by years of extraction.
   Routed through the ordinary _mutate machinery so the chronicle shows
   the change.

5. RULE STYLE — POLICY FROM PERSONALITY
   There is no policy AI. Each year, each polity scores five facets from
   its leader's traits plus situation, and the dominant facet(s) fire:

   - BENEVOLENT (Righteous +2, Humble +1, Loyal +1, Scholarly +1):
     prosperity +0.4, leader standing up, karma +1, no personal gain.
     Good rulers die respected and poor.
   - EXTRACTIVE (Greedy +2, Power-Hungry +2): prosperity -0.5, leader
     resources +2..4, karma -1. Overtaxing, seized land, monopolies.
   - CRUEL (Cruel +2, Bully +1, Bloodthirsty +1, Ruthless +1):
     prosperity -0.4, unrest +2, karma -2, and seeds grudge(ruler) in
     living cultivators whose home lies in the territory.
   - NEGLECTFUL (Broken +2, Cold +1, Ascetic +1, or mortal ruler past
     ~70): prosperity -0.2, unrest +1. Petitions unread, granaries
     unrepaired.
   - CONSCRIPTION (only at war, or Bloodthirsty): prosperity -0.3,
     unrest +1, army +N for war resolution (§9).

   Unrest decays by 1 in a benevolent or neutral year. Every fired facet
   writes a concrete chronicle-ready line: "King Batu of the Wolf Steppe
   tripled the herd-tax; the valleys go hungry."

6. EDICTS — THE SENSELESS RULES
   Separate from the facets, a yearly edict roll with chance proportional
   to Proud + Cold + vice-trait count. An edict is drawn from a data table
   of templates: total silence after dusk; all subjects must wear the
   ruler's color; music banned; laughter taxed; mirrors smashed; commoners
   may be addressed only by title, never by name; mandatory worship of a
   god of a random OTHER land ("the Salt Mother of the Salt Wastes",
   "the Sky Wolf of the Wolf Steppe") — the nine lands make "a foreign
   god" concrete.

   Each active edict: prosperity -0.2/year, unrest +1/year. At most 3
   active per polity. Edicts persist until the ruler changes or a
   non-Stubborn ruler repeals one in a good year; proclamation and repeal
   are both logged. Edicts are the layer's main readability payoff — they
   make a bad reign describable in one sentence.

7. VIRTUE, VICE, AND KARMA
   TRAIT_POOL gains four vice traits: Bully, Power-Hungry, Cruel,
   Bloodthirsty. All get action weights, duel behavior, and mutation
   paths:

   - Bully picks fights only DOWNWARD (targets a realm below — the
     tyranny of realms inverted), shakes down juniors for resources, and
     hands every victim a grudge and a little insight. Crushed in public,
     a Bully becomes Broken or Humble.
   - Power-Hungry schemes (socialize-heavy), covets thrones and sect
     headship; passed over in a succession, becomes Vengeful.
   - Cruel maims defeated opponents (victim gains a maim epithet and
     insight; the sim generates walking evidence).
   - Bloodthirsty escalates duels to lethal and volunteers for wars;
     surviving near-death turns them Cautious or Ascetic.

   Moral sets: VIRTUE = {Righteous, Humble, Loyal};
   VICE = {Cruel, Bully, Power-Hungry, Bloodthirsty}.
   Greedy, Ruthless, Vengeful, Cold stay morally gray — no karma weight
   (Greedy and Ruthless keep their existing resource edges; the gray
   traits remain PC-legal).

   KARMA is an integer on every agent, seeded 2*(virtue traits) -
   2*(vice traits), then moved by DEEDS, which dominate over disposition
   in a long life: killing the defenseless -2, cruel or extractive rule
   per §5, sparing a beaten foe +1, rescue or liberation +2, dying in
   defense of others +3 (posthumous; it flavors the obituary and the
   grief of friends).

   Couplings — the whole moral economy, all mechanical:
   - LUCK: each year fortune drifts one step toward sign(karma), on top
     of the existing streaky luck. Tribulation success gets +karma/4
     percentage points, clamped to +/-5. The adventure table tilts: high
     karma finds fateful encounters, low karma finds ambushes.
   - RESOURCES: agents with vice traits take +1 resource from every
     victorious contest and successful adventure (spoils to the bone),
     and vice rulers extract per §5. Evil is the fast lane for wealth;
     virtue is the luck lane. Idealists break through; tyrants get rich.
   - GRUDGES: grudges accrue at 1.5x intensity against negative-karma
     agents. The wronged remember.

8. THE CAMERA CONSTRAINT (the one deliberate cheat)
   The PC never rolls, and never mutates into, the four VICE traits;
   neither does any agent currently bound to the PC as friend, sworn,
   lover, master, or disciple. Blocked mutations reroute to Vengeful,
   Cold, or Broken — the viewpoint cast can darken, but not become
   monstrous.

   This is a camera constraint, not plot armor — the same kind of
   exception as PC selection itself. It protects the reader's seat, not
   the characters: PC and friends still lose, stall, get maimed, and die
   on schedule. Documented here as the layer's only breach of SIM FIRST.

9. CONSEQUENCE EVENTS (event-phase additions)
   - REVOLT: when a polity's unrest passes REVOLT_THRESHOLD, roll yearly.
     The revolt seeks a CHAMPION: a living cultivator with a grudge
     against the ruler, or a Righteous one whose home lies in the
     territory; failing that, a leaderless uprising (weak). Resolution is
     a contest under the tyranny of realms: a mortal tyrant falls to any
     realm-2 champion; a cultivator-king turns revolt into massacre
     (prosperity and ruler karma collapse, survivors gain insight and
     grudges — adversity, as ever, is the insight engine). On success the
     champion is offered the throne — accepting is the ruler exit;
     declining installs a local notable — unrest resets, and the epithet
     "Tyrant-Breaker" is stamped.
   - ASSASSINATION: a ruler at karma <= -4 attracts attempts from
     grudge-holders within one realm. Cautious defends; a success is a
     succession with a corpse.
   - WAR: between edge-adjacent sovereigns (rarely corner). Triggered by
     Bloodthirsty or Power-Hungry rulers with an army, or by contested
     successions. An abstract 1-3 year campaign: prosperity falls on both
     sides, conscripts die (chronicle color, not agents), cultivators may
     join (action-phase nudge for Bloodthirsty, Greedy, and Loyal
     natives; battlefield deaths, insight, and spoils). Outcomes: tribute,
     vassalization, or a region changes hands.
   - PETITION: a settlement below prosperity 3 petitions a sect. This is
     the sect/polity interface: a mission hook answered by Righteous or
     standing-hungry members — an adventure variant in that land (drive
     off the tax collectors, confront the magistrate, kill the beast the
     ruler ignored). Success lifts prosperity and karma and standing, and
     makes an enemy of the polity.
   - ADVENTURE DESTINATIONS: every adventure now rolls a destination land
     (weighted toward the Middle Plain and its edge neighbors). The
     destination's prosperity reshapes the risk table: misruled lands
     yield bandits, refugees to rescue, and the tyrant's men; golden
     lands yield auctions, fairs, and patrons.
   - SECULAR SUCCESSION: on a ruler's death, the seat goes to a courtier
     (a generated mortal notable) unless an ambitious cultivator claims
     it — stalled, aging, Power-Hungry, or Greedy agents weigh in;
     Righteous ones only when the land is visibly suffering (the idealist
     takeover). Contested successions can escalate to coups and wars.

10. INTAKES AND THE COMMON PEOPLE
   Commoners never become agents; their lives reach the sim at the
   recruitment gate. Each recruit rolls a home settlement in their
   homeland. From a misruled home (prosperity <= 3): fewer starting
   resources, +1 burden, +2 insight — adversity arrives pre-banked — and
   a seeded grudge(home ruler) at intensity 1-2. From a prosperous home:
   more starting resources and zero insight. The sheltered-genius /
   battered-underdog divergence now begins before year one, and some
   careers carry a political grudge from birth.

   Family stipend: agents at realm <= 2 draw +1 resource/year while their
   home settlement sits at prosperity >= 7. A home under conscription may
   instead seed a fresh grudge (a brother taken for the levies).

11. SECTS UNDER THE POLITICS LAYER
   Sect heads keep cultivating — headship is rulership-lite, not an exit.
   The head's virtue/vice tilts the sect's richness multiplier a few
   points either way and drifts junior disciples' standing; under a
   vice-heavy head, Righteous or Humble disciples may DEFECT to another
   sect (a new voluntary-exit flavor that seeds cross-sect relationships
   and future feud fuel). Everything else goes through petitions (§9).

12. LOGGING, TAGS, COMMANDS
   World.log gains an optional `place=` argument. Tag priority becomes:
   [PC] > relationship tags > [home] (the event's place lies in the PC's
   home region or land) > [world] > [famous]. The [home] tag is what
   keeps the PC's homeland politics on screen without flooding the
   chronicle: the reader watches one land's fortunes because it is THEIR
   land.

   World-event lines are restricted to sovereign-level politics:
   coronations, depositions, wars, revolts, land-scale edicts. Village
   texture stays in the private histories of those it touches. All
   political lines follow the house style: past tense, concrete,
   self-contained.

   New commands: `map` (the 3x3 grid with prosperity words per land),
   `land NAME` (one land's tree: polities, rulers, active edicts,
   prosperity), `courts` (every ruler: polity, type, style word, unrest).
   `sheet` shows home and karma. Obituaries mention reigns and thrones
   refused. The final report adds "The State of the Nine Lands".

13. TUNING TARGETS (additions to Part III's)
   - At any moment, 2-4 of the nine lands are under visibly bad rule and
     at least one is prosperous-to-golden. Misery is common, not
     universal.
   - 2-5% of each cohort ends its story on a throne; most rulers remain
     mortal notables. Rulership is a real exit, not a common one.
   - A revolt or war somewhere every 8-15 years; assassinations rarer.
   - KARMA CHECK: sort the dead by karma. High-karma agents should show
     slightly better final realms and kinder deaths; low-karma agents
     more wealth and more violent ends. Neither monotone — a saint must
     sometimes die young and a tyrant sometimes die old and rich, or the
     karma system has become dramaturgy.
   - CHRONICLE BALANCE: political lines at most ~30% of the chronicle.
   - The old targets still hold: the funnel, talent-vs-luck, and
     distinguishability — now including `log <ruler>` reading like a
     reign, and the log of a recruit from a misruled land showing the
     land in the life.

14. IMPLEMENTATION SESSIONS
   Seven sessions; each leaves the sim runnable, seeded-reproducible, and
   is validated by reading logs before the next begins. Guardrails
   throughout: single file, stdlib only, all randomness via world.rng,
   every event through World.log with honest flags, every constant in the
   data tables at the top.

   SESSION 1 — GEOGRAPHY. Place dataclass and world tree; nine lands on
   the grid; the two doubled pools and secondary land names; Middle Plain
   as melting pot; homeland -> home settlement; recruitment weights by
   grid position (retire the static pool weights); settlement naming;
   `map` command. Prosperity exists but sits at baseline. No behavior
   change — validate that a seeded run is unchanged in spirit and names
   still read well.

   SESSION 2 — POLITIES AND MORTAL RULE. Polity dataclass and worldgen;
   mortal ruler agents (court-skewed traits, `ruling`, `karma` as a
   tracked-but-uncoupled field); the rule-style engine (§5); edicts (§6);
   prosperity and unrest dynamics; tribute and mandate propagation;
   mortal successions; `courts` and `land` commands; [home] tag and
   `place=` logging. Validate by reading 50 years of political chronicle:
   2-4 bad lands, edicts legible, no flooding.

   SESSION 3 — CONTACT SURFACE. Recruit home settlements and intake
   effects (§10); family stipends; adventure destinations with
   prosperity-shaped risk tables; petitions to sects. Validate: the log
   of a recruit from a misruled land shows the land in the life.

   SESSION 4 — RULERSHIP AS EXIT. The RULE action and cultivation lock;
   cultivator claims in successions; usurpation; invitation and refusal;
   abdication; power-corrupts mutation; follow-pc and PC camera extended
   to reigns (the camera keeps rolling on a PC throne). Validate: 2-5% of
   a cohort ends enthroned; `log <ruler>` reads like a reign.

   SESSION 5 — KARMA AND VICE. The four vice traits everywhere (pool,
   action weights, duel behavior, mutations); VIRTUE/VICE sets; karma
   deeds and couplings (luck drift, tribulation modifier, adventure
   tilt, vice resource bonus, grudge multiplier); the camera constraint
   (§8). Validate: karma check and talent-vs-luck target both hold.

   SESSION 6 — CONSEQUENCES. Revolts with champions; assassinations;
   wars along grid edges with territory and vassalage changes; sect-head
   tilt and defections. Validate: a revolt or war every 8-15 years;
   funnel intact.

   SESSION 7 — TUNING AND POLISH. Full 200-year runs against every
   target in §13 and Part III; final report's "State of the Nine Lands";
   obituary and epithet polish; update CLAUDE.md. Validate: pick five
   agents including one ruler and one revolt champion — every life
   describable in one sentence.


================================================================================
PART VII — THE PLAYABLE LAYER (seasons, stances, autocombat, the demon front:
full spec)
================================================================================

What this layer adds: the game. The PC stops being only a camera and becomes
agent 65 played by a human — same rules, same funnel, no plot armor. Play
runs at SEASON resolution while the world keeps its year tick; fights unfold
into rounds with stances and pause points; a permanent demon front gives the
world a source of deathly fighting that never runs dry; masters, professions
and techniques give the player long-term projects. Everything here obeys
Part IV's law: WHAT CHANGES IS RESOLUTION, NOT PHYSICS.

Decisions taken up front (so no session relitigates them):
- NO VICE LOCK ON THE PLAYER. The camera constraint (VI §8) is repealed for
  the PC in play mode: the player may earn, mutate into, and act on vice
  traits; karma and grudges are the honest price. Bound companions KEEP the
  constraint — the supporting cast still darkens rather than turning
  monstrous, because the reader's seat around the player is still a seat.
- DEMONS ARE A FIELD, NOT AGENTS. Like the commoners (VI §10), the demon
  host is a number plus scene tables — a threat level on one edge of the
  map, never an agent roster. Named demon lords as real agents are a future
  session, deliberately out of scope.
- ONE CODE PATH. The year loop is restructured as plan-then-play (an agenda
  rolled at year start, executed season by season) in ALL modes; play mode
  merely pauses for input between seasons. Batch runs stay seeded-
  reproducible against themselves; compatibility with pre-VII seeds is
  statistical (all tuning targets re-validated), not bitwise.
- NO THROUGHPUT EDGE. A season's payout is one quarter of the corresponding
  yearly action. Four choices a year instead of one is the player's whole
  advantage: they can REACT to the agenda. An autopilot player must land
  inside the Part III funnel (§13 below).
- REIGNS RUN AT YEAR TEMPO even for the player. The court eats the
  calendar: a played throne offers one decision per year, not four.
- PROFESSIONS AND TECHNIQUES ARE PLAYER-FACING ONLY in v1, with one small
  exception (famous NPCs roll token techniques, §9) so the player is not
  uniquely armed. NPC resource abstraction (Part III) is unchanged.


1. TWO CLOCKS — THE YEAR KERNEL AND THE SEASON SURFACE
   The world thinks in years; the player lives in seasons (spring, summer,
   autumn, winter). `World.step()` becomes three parts:

   - PLAN: at year start, roll the YEAR AGENDA — every event-phase event
     that will fire this year is decided NOW and stamped with a season:
     tournaments (autumn), expeditions, feuds, musters and campaign years
     (summer), petitions, incursions, ripening grudges aimed at the PC.
     The agenda is the trick that makes "stop BEFORE something interesting"
     possible: the engine knows the near future because it rolled it.
   - PLAY: four season sub-steps. NPCs still take ONE action per year
     (rolled in spring, read as "what they mostly did that year"); agenda
     events resolve in their stamped season; the PC acts every season.
   - CLOSE: the resolution phase (prosperity drift, stipends, karma luck,
     breakthroughs, aging, exits) and intakes, unchanged, at winter's end.

   In batch and observer modes the three parts run in one call and nothing
   changes on screen. In play mode (`--play`) the loop pauses each season
   for the player's choice. Player UI input never touches `world.rng`;
   different play diverges a seeded world, identical play replays it.

2. THE PLAYER CHARACTER
   `--play` adds agent 65 to the watched intake: talent, traits, homeland,
   home settlement all rolled honestly (the funnel is real; there is no
   point-buy). The player names them and may pick sex and homeland; that is
   all. New PC-only state: hp (in-fight), wound level, stance
   proficiencies, proficiencies (Body / Weapon / Theory), profession ranks,
   techniques, pills and toxicity, standing orders (§6).

   Traits stop weighting the PC's action choice (the player chooses) but
   keep every other job: power modifiers, event outcomes, mutation. DEEDS
   NOW DRIVE MUTATION for the PC: fight murderous three times in five
   years and the Bloodthirsty roll comes for you; spare three beaten foes
   and Righteous does. The world writes on the player exactly as it writes
   on everyone.

   Death: the obituary prints, then a choice — end the run with the final
   report, or continue playing the successor `_succeed_pc` picks. Voluntary
   exits are never rolled for the player, only offered when the conditions
   fire. Thrones are offered as real choices (claims, invitations, revolt
   championships); a played reign is §10.

3. SEASONS, ACTIVITIES, TIMESKIP
   One activity per season, chosen from a menu; Enter repeats the current
   activity. Every activity pays in the existing currencies (qi, insight,
   resources, standing, karma, burden, rels) plus the player-layer ones,
   at ONE QUARTER of the corresponding yearly action's rate.

   THE ACTIVITY LIST (v1; the payout column is the design intent, the
   constants live in the data tables):

   - Cultivate at the sect ... qi (cultivate/4), +resources trickle
   - Meditate in retreat ..... qi (seclude/4); relationships decay
   - Train the body .......... Body proficiency; +max hp, wound resistance
   - Train a weapon .......... Weapon proficiency; combat bonus; stance
                               proficiency progress
   - Sect training hall ...... learn techniques from the sect library
                               (standing + resources gate); stance teaching
   - Study theory ............ Theory proficiency; breakthrough bonus,
                               insight trickle
   - Fight injustice ......... pick a misruled land; the harsh-road
                               adventure table + petition-flavored scenes;
                               karma up, grudge(ruler) both ways
   - Hunt spirit beasts ...... beast risk table; materials (resources /
                               crafting stock), injury risk, insight on
                               near-death
   - Trade run ............... pick two lands; margin scales with their
                               prosperity gap; road risk en route; the
                               PROFITEER option (grain into a starving
                               land at famine prices) pays double and
                               costs karma — vice stays the fast lane
   - Seek a master ........... search + trial scene; a master rel gives a
                               teaching multiplier and grants stances /
                               techniques (§8)
   - Socialize ............... rels, standing, scheming (socialize/4)
   - The demon front ......... §7; the deadliest lane in the game
   - Join the muster ......... when a war or levy exists (VI §9); the
                               player gets the choice `_take_service`
                               rolls for NPCs
   - Heal / brew / forge ..... profession seasons (§8), once learned

   TIMESKIP is an activity with a duration: "keep doing X for up to N
   seasons (cap 12), wake me if". The engine plans a year, checks the
   agenda for interrupts, and stops THE SEASON BEFORE the event — the eve.
   Retreat timeskips carry seclusion's full social cost.

   THE INTERRUPT TABLE (personal, never world news — a war two lands over
   goes in the digest, or no skip would ever finish):

   HARD (always wake, on the eve):
   - a tournament the PC's realm band gathers for
   - breakthrough ready (qi 100 + insight met); any tribulation
   - a challenge, ambush, or grudge against the PC ripening
   - war, muster, or incursion touching the PC's home land, sect, or a
     front they served on within 3 years
   - a master's offer; an assigned petition; an expedition draw
   - death, maiming, or betrayal of anyone in `rels` at intensity >= 2
   - the home settlement falling below desperate (prosperity < 2)
   - a throne offered, or the PC's ruler deposed/killed
   SOFT (never wake; summarized): everything else the chronicle prints.

   On waking (or completing), print the DIGEST: seasons elapsed, gains,
   the missed chronicle filtered through the existing [PC]/[home]/[world]
   machinery, then the interrupting event framed on its eve.

4. STANCES — EDGE x MANNER
   A stance is one EDGE (required) plus at most one MANNER. The two-slot
   grammar is the whole combination rule: every sensible pair from the
   idea list exists, and contradictions (murderous non-violence) cannot
   be expressed.

   EDGES — how far the fight goes. Sets the stop threshold and what can
   kill or maim:

     edge        stops at      kill                maim        modifier
     Sparring    75% hp left   never               2% accident none
     Duelling    50% hp left   2% accident         5%          none
     All-out     yield/K.O.    10% accidental      15%         +15% damage
     Murderous   death         intended            —           -10% to hit
                                                               (telegraphed)

   In All-out and Murderous a beaten foe may YIELD; the victor chooses:
   spare (+1 karma, the existing KARMA_SPARE) or execute (-2 karma via
   `_karma_kill` — killing the defenseless, exactly as the kernel already
   prices it). Murderous carries a standing cost when witnessed: people
   remember who came to kill.

   MANNERS — how you fight and what the fight is FOR:

     Rage        +20% damage dealt and taken
     Patience    -15% for two rounds, +15% after
     Harmonious  no modifier; +10% against Rage and vice-trait opponents
     Showy       -10%; victory pays +1 standing (face)
     Humiliating -10%; the defeated loses standing and the grudge lands
                 heavier (+1 intensity, on top of the karma multiplier)
     Studying    -15%; +1 insight win or lose, and stance/technique
                 progress
     Merciful    -10%; kill and maim chances are forced to zero (the
                 non-violent pledge, expressed as a manner so it can
                 temper any edge)

   PROFICIENCY: each edge and manner has a rank 0-3. Untrained (0) doubles
   the stance's malus and halves its bonus; ranks come from use, masters,
   and the training hall. Stances are teachable content — a master's
   parting gift can be Patience at rank 2.

   THE KERNEL SPEAKS STANCE TOO. `_duel` is rewritten to pick both
   fighters' stances from traits — Bloodthirsty goes Murderous, Cruel
   Humiliating, Proud Showy, Cautious Patience, Scholarly Studying,
   Righteous Duelling/Merciful, the rest Duelling — and resolves through
   the same outcome constants. This replaces today's hardcoded
   trait-to-lethality logic with a vocabulary the chronicle can print
   ("fought in a rage", "waited out the storm"), and MUST be retuned so
   aggregate NPC death and maim rates match the session-7 numbers.

5. COMBAT — ONE DISTRIBUTION, TWO RESOLUTIONS
   The kernel's one-roll duel (win chance pa/(pa+pb)) remains the truth.
   Fights stay one-roll off camera; a fight the PC is in unfolds into
   rounds. THE INVARIANT: the round model's win probability must sit
   within 3 percentage points of pa/(pa+pb) across the matchup grid, so
   the funnel cannot tell which resolution ran.

   - THE TYRANNY OF REALMS IS UNTOUCHED. A gap of 1+ realms never reaches
     rounds: the existing flee-or-die logic runs, stances irrelevant.
     Nothing in this layer — stances, proficiencies, gear, techniques —
     may bridge a realm. The hard cap: all player-layer combat bonuses
     sum to at most +4 power, less than half a realm's 10.
   - ROUNDS: both sides enter at 100% hp (less standing wounds). Each
     round is an exchange weighted by power x stance modifiers; the loser
     of the exchange takes 12-20% hp scaled by the power ratio. Tuned to
     resolve equal fights in 3-8 rounds — longer and Patience turns
     degenerate, shorter and the pauses never fire.
   - PAUSES (the autocombat contract — no round-by-round decisions):
     own hp crossing 50% and 25%: yield / switch edge or manner / fight
     on / in lethal fights, attempt escape (base 50%, up with Movement
     technique and Cautious, down a realm gap). Enemy hp crossing 50%
     and 25% while ahead: downgrade the edge or switch manner (the
     moment to go Humiliating, Studying, or Merciful). One prompt per
     crossing, then the fight flows. NPCs hit the same thresholds and
     switch by trait rules; the player may also pre-empt all prompts
     with standing orders (§6).
   - WOUNDS, NOT HIT POINTS. hp exists only inside a fight. Walking out
     under 50% leaves a LIGHT wound (-10 max hp), under 25% a SERIOUS
     one (-25 max hp, halves activity payouts). Wounds heal one level
     per restful season, instantly under a healer or healing pill.
     Maiming remains what it is: permanent, an epithet, the kernel's
     `_maim`.
   - THE TEST HARNESS the thresholds asked for: `--test-combat` runs a
     stdlib Monte Carlo (10k fights per cell) over realm/talent/stance
     matchups and prints: win% vs the one-roll formula (must hold the
     invariant), round-count distribution, and death/maim rates per edge
     (sparring deaths 0; duel accidents < 3%; maim ordered sparring <
     duelling < all-out). The 75/50 stop lines and pause thresholds are
     data-table constants tuned against this harness, not guesses.

6. STANDING ORDERS
   So fights interrupt only when it matters, the player keeps a small
   card of defaults: default edge and manner, auto-yield threshold
   (default 25%), execution policy (spare / ask / kill), escape policy.
   A fight that resolves entirely inside standing orders prints as one
   summary line, like any NPC fight. Orders are exactly what NPC traits
   are to NPCs — which is why the machinery is shared.

7. THE DEMON FRONT (the minimal permanent war)
   At worldgen one outer EDGE of the 3x3 grid (seeded roll) borders the
   DEMON WASTE. The 2-3 lands along it are MARCH-LANDS; the front is a
   fact of their geography, like weather.

   - `world.demon_threat`, float 0-10, starts at 3. Drift: +0.15/year.
     Every cultivator-season served on the front: -0.1. The front is a
     pot that boils unless people die stirring it.
   - THE FRONT ACTIVITY: a season of deadly fighting — a risk table
     roughly twice as lethal as the harsh road, paying insight, standing,
     materials, and front epithets (Demon-Scarred, Wastewalker). NPCs
     volunteer by the war-volunteer machinery (Righteous and Bloodthirsty
     skew, march-land natives doubled) — the front burns through NPC
     lives too, honestly.
   - INCURSION: while threat >= 7, roll ~35% a year (agenda-stamped, so
     a timeskip stops on its eve for march-landers and front veterans).
     A one-year event: march-land prosperity -1.5, conscript deaths as
     chronicle color, defenders drawn like an expedition (plus march
     polity armies), resolved as a contest against the threat number.
     Victory: threat resets to 2-4, survivors gain insight and standing,
     the dead are named. Defeat (rare): a march settlement is SWALLOWED —
     prosperity floored, a permanent scar line in `state_of_the_lands`,
     threat stays high. Either way `_remember` files it.
   - March-lands below prosperity 3 petition the sects for front relief
     through the existing petition machinery. No new diplomacy: the
     Waste holds no court, takes no tribute, signs nothing.

8. MASTERS, PROFICIENCIES, PROFESSIONS
   - PROFICIENCIES (Body / Weapon / Theory): rank 0-5; rank N costs N
     seasons of the matching activity. Effects deliberately small (hp
     and wound resistance; combat bonus inside the +4 cap; breakthrough
     percentage points) — they are texture and project, not power.
   - SEEK A MASTER: a search season finds a candidate (realm >= PC+1,
     compatible traits weighted); their TRIAL is a scene — a test of the
     PC's record (karma, insight, epithets, a fight at Sparring). A won
     master rel: teaching multiplier on training seasons, stance grants,
     occasionally a technique; and a real relationship the world can
     kill, betray, or avenge.
   - PROFESSIONS (alchemy / forging / healing): ONE rank mechanic, three
     skins. Rank 0-3; rank N needs 3xN practice seasons, rank 2+ needs a
     teacher or manual.
       Alchemy: brew Qi pills (+qi on a cultivation season), Healing
       pills (heal a wound level), Clarity pills (+3%/rank on one
       breakthrough attempt). TOXICITY: every pill taken +1; above 6,
       each further pill +1 BURDEN — pill-stacking gives power now and
       poisons the ceiling later, exactly Part I §3's promise made
       mechanical. Toxicity decays 1 per pill-free year.
       Forging: gear at +1/+2 power (inside the cap), sellable.
       Healing: heal own wounds free; healing others pays karma,
       gratitude rels, and at rank 2+, a LIFE-DEBT rel when the patient
       would have died — the social ledger's strongest coin.
     Pills and gear the player sells melt into NPC `resources`; NPCs do
     not practice professions in v1 (their single resource number is
     already the abstraction of all of this).

9. TECHNIQUES (minimal)
   A technique is a card: school, realm gate, one effect. Known or not —
   comprehension levels are deferred. Schools:
   - MOVEMENT: +15% escape per rank of the school's ladder (realm 1+)
   - MISSILES: a free first-round hit at range (realm 2+)
   - PRESSURE: overawe mortals and realm-lower foes without a fight;
     scene intimidation (realm 3+)
   - FLIGHT: escape almost anything; travel color; the genre gate at
     realm 3+
   - ELEMENTAL ARTS: +1 power, elemental epithets and scene flavor
     (realm 2+)
   Acquisition: the sect training hall (standing + resources), masters,
   expedition and adventure treasure slots, and looted MANUALS — 20% of
   which are FLAWED: the technique works, and learning it adds +1 burden
   discovered only at the next tribulation. Everything counts inside the
   +4 cap. Famous NPCs (realm >= FAME_REALM or standing >= 10) roll 0-2
   techniques at realm-up so the arms race is symmetric.

10. A PLAYED THRONE
   Throne paths reach the player as choices: press a claim, accept or
   refuse an invitation, champion a revolt, keep or lay down the crown.
   A played reign runs at YEAR tempo: each year the player picks one
   facet to emphasize from the top three their traits score (the engine
   fires it alongside what the situation forces), and may repeal or
   proclaim an edict when the engine's own rules would allow one. That
   is all v1 offers — rulership stays rulership-shaped: cultivation
   locked, qi frozen, insight only from governance adversity, corruption
   rolls unmodified. Abdication is always on the menu, with VI §4's
   consequences. Playing a king should feel like holding a job that is
   slowly eating your cultivation — because it is.

11. UI (terminal, v1)
   The season prompt: date line (Year 12, autumn — age 26, Foundation,
   qi 61, insight 9, burden 2), resources/standing/karma-word, wound if
   any, active rels one line, agenda notices ("the sects gather in
   autumn for the tournament"). Then the numbered menu, Enter to repeat,
   `skip N doing X`, `orders`, `bag` (pills, gear, techniques,
   proficiencies, toxicity), and every existing observer command (`map`,
   `courts`, `log`, `sheet`...). Fights print one line per round, pause
   menus at thresholds, a summary line when standing orders carried it.
   Every player scene routes through `World.log` with honest flags —
   the PC tag machinery already knows what to show.

12. TUNING TARGETS (additions; every earlier target still holds)
   - AUTOPILOT FUNNEL: 32 seeded runs where the PC is played by a random
     legal-choice bot must land inside the Part III funnel and die at
     ordinary rates. The player's edge is judgment, not existence.
   - COMBAT INVARIANT: round-model win% within 3pp of pa/(pa+pb);
     equal fights 3-8 rounds; sparring kills nobody; duel accidents
     < 3%; maim rates ordered by edge. (`--test-combat` prints the
     whole table.)
