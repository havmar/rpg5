================================================================================
CULTIVATION WORLD SIMULATOR — DESIGN DOCUMENT
A wuxia/xianxia career simulation with an AI DM as narrative renderer
================================================================================

CORE PREMISE
------------
The game is a simulation kernel with a narrative lens on top. The world runs as
a mechanical process (Python scripts); the AI DM's job is to read the logs and
tell you what they meant. Design order: SIM FIRST, CHEAT LATER. No narrative
gravity, no plot armor, no dramaturgy in version one. If the raw event logs of
individual agents already read like lives, the kernel is right, and any later
AI narration is a renderer over honest data rather than fiction papering over
noise.

The base deliverable is not a game but a world generator: take a starting
class of ~64 students across several sects and simulate their careers over
centuries until a few reach the top. The generations above (elders) and below
(future intakes) are simulated by the same rules. A player character is added
later as "agent 65" — same rules, same funnel — with a richer, game-like
resource layer unfolded only for them.

An elegant loop follows from this: run the simulation for 200 years first,
and the survivors ARE the elders, rivals, and legends of the world the player
is born into. Every campaign world is the fossil record of a previous
simulation.


================================================================================
PART I — THE GENRE FRAME (what the mechanics must reproduce)
================================================================================

1. TWO AXES INSTEAD OF ONE POWER STAT
   - REALM: the visible power tier (Qi Condensation -> Foundation
     Establishment -> Core Formation -> Nascent Soul -> ...). Defines what a
     character can do.
   - DAO HEART / INSIGHT: self-mastery, conviction, understanding of one's
     personal path. Determines whether a character CAN advance and survive
     advancing. Realm is loud; insight is the actual engine of careers.

2. TIERED POWER, EMBRACED RATHER THAN BALANCED
   Cultivation fiction is explicitly unbalanced, and the design encodes it:
   - Same realm: genuine contest.
   - One realm apart: the weaker side can only survive, flee, delay — unless
     they bring narrative assets (treasures, formations, ambush, allies,
     sacrifice, a hidden trump card).
   - Two realms apart: not a fight at all.
   This "tyranny of realms" makes power gaps feel real and makes acquiring
   trump cards and allies a central strategic activity — exactly how the
   genre's protagonists operate.

3. BREAKTHROUGH AS EVENT, NOT ACCUMULATION
   Advancement requires three distinct things:
   - QI: grindable (time, spirit stones, pills, good environments).
   - INSIGHT: non-grindable; earned only through lived adversity — defeats,
     near-death, betrayal, loss, epiphany, acts true to one's dao.
   - FOUNDATION QUALITY: how cleanly previous stages were built; shortcuts
     (pill-stacking) give power now and poison the ceiling later.
   The breakthrough itself is a dramatic set-piece (tribulation) with real
   failure states: qi deviation, heart demons taking root, crippled
   cultivation, death.

4. THE DAO AND ITS SHADOW
   Each character has a personal dao — a principle, not just an element
   ("the sword that never lies", "the dao of endurance"). Acting in accord
   with it generates insight; betraying it plants HEART DEMONS that stay
   dormant and ambush the character at the next tribulation. Personality
   flaws are literally the progression bottleneck.

5. TIME AND LIFESPAN AS THE DEEP ECONOMY
   Careers span 50–300 years. Each realm extends lifespan, creating a slow
   clock: stall too long and you die of old age before the next breakthrough.
   The constant tension: seclusion (qi, safety, but time burns and the world
   moves on) versus the world (insight, resources, grudges, danger).
   Time-skips are first-class; rivals also cultivate offscreen.

6. THE SOCIAL LEDGER
   Face, favors, karma, sect standing, oaths, sworn brotherhood, life-debts.
   Grudges are tracked as living entities: humiliate a young master today
   and his Nascent Soul grandfather is a problem in twenty years. Killing
   solves problems but generates karma; sparing enemies generates future
   threats. Often more consequential than combat.

7. SUPPORTING SUBSYSTEMS (later, mostly player-facing)
   - Techniques as collectible, comprehension-leveled knowledge; incomplete
     or flawed manuals as risk/reward traps.
   - Pills with toxicity buildup and diminishing returns.
   - "Fortuitous encounters" as a luck currency the game spends on agents.
   - Scale zoom per realm tier: village stakes -> sect politics -> empires
     -> the heavens.

   The unifying idea: the system simulates not "getting stronger" but THE
   PRICE of getting stronger — time, attachments, integrity, and the people
   left behind or made into enemies.


================================================================================
PART II — SIMULATION ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================

1. UNIFORM AGENT MODEL (the chosen approach)
   Everyone — elders, the focal cohort, juniors — runs identical rules at
   full(ish) fidelity. Generations emerge from recruitment and death, not
   from special code. Only the eventual PLAYER gets a deluxe layer (real
   inventory, techniques, scene-level play); NPCs compress all of that into
   a single resource score. Population equilibrium of ~150–300 agents at one
   action and a handful of events per agent-year is computationally trivial.

   (A three-band fidelity system — full sim for the cohort, institutional
   sim for elders, statistical pools below — was considered and set aside;
   it becomes relevant only if the world scales far beyond a few hundred
   agents. Keep the idea in reserve: promotion/demotion between bands, where
   an irrelevant agent degrades to coarse simulation and an exceptional
   statistical youngster gets instantiated as a full agent.)

2. TICK STRUCTURE
   Base clock: one year (careers span centuries; finer ticks drown in
   noise). Run 200–300 years per world. Discrete EVENTS layered over the
   ticks (tournaments, expeditions, feuds, successions) are where
   trajectories diverge; the years between are accumulation and drift.

3. THE CAREER ENGINE
   Careers emerge from the interaction of fixed endowment (talent), evolving
   personality (traits), relationships, and streaky luck. Because insight
   comes only from adversity actually experienced, the sim naturally
   reproduces the genre's core irony with no scripting: the sheltered genius
   who plateaus at their first real tribulation; the battered underdog who
   breaks through.

4. ATTRITION AS THE SHAPE OF THE STORY
   Target funnel per intake of 64, over a century-plus:
     ~35–45 reach realm 2, ~15 reach realm 3, ~4–6 reach realm 4,
     0–2 ever touch the top.
   Deaths need textured causes: tribulations, expeditions, feuds, qi
   deviation, war, old age. Crucially, include VOLUNTARY EXITS — students
   who marry out, take administrative posts, lose conviction. A funnel that
   only kills feels like a meat grinder; one where people also choose
   ordinary lives feels like a world.

5. THE LAYER ABOVE AS WEATHER
   Elders generate the environment the cohort climbs through: which sect is
   ascendant, which resources are contested, wars, power vacuums. They are
   also the opportunity-injection engine: a dying elder seeks an heir, war
   creates battlefield promotions, a purge orphans talented students. The
   top of the world needs TEMPO — shifts every 10–30 years — so the cohort
   never climbs a static ladder.

6. RANDOMNESS WITH MEMORY (post-toy refinements)
   Pure dice produce noise-shaped careers. Two correctives:
   - DESTINY HOOKS: each agent rolls a small number at creation (hidden
     bloodline, doomed love, an old family enemy); the event system
     preferentially fires on them. Arc-shaped lives without predetermined
     outcomes.
   - STREAKY LUCK: fortune compounds in this genre (treasures attract
     treasures, attention attracts danger); let recent outcomes bias event
     selection slightly.

7. EVENT LOGGING AS THE NARRATIVE SUBSTRATE
   Every consequential outcome writes a structured entry: year, actors,
   event, outcome, relationship deltas, WITNESSES. Log rumors as well as
   facts — cultivation drama runs on who knows what, and a rumor layer lets
   the DM generate misunderstandings, hidden identities, and reveals
   honestly from the data. The simulation says:
     "Y3 autumn: Student 17 defeated Student 41 in the tournament final;
      41's backer lost face; 41 gained grudge(17); 17 gained reputation +2
      and the attention of Elder Wu."
   The AI DM turns that into the scene.


================================================================================
PART III — THE TOY VERSION (first buildable artifact, ~300–500 lines)
================================================================================

One uniform agent model, one loop, three generations alive at once.

1. AGENT SHEET (~a dozen fields)
   - age
   - realm (1–6) and qi progress within realm (0–100)
   - talent (1–10, fixed at birth): multiplier on qi gain, soft cap on realm
   - insight (banked from lived events; required to break through)
   - burden (one counter standing in for heart demons / unresolved baggage)
   - resources (ONE number for wealth/pills/treasures; the player version
     later explodes this into real inventory — NPCs never need it)
   - sect, and standing within it
   - traits: 2–3 from a small pool
   - relationships: sparse list (rival, friend, master, lover, grudge),
     each with an intensity
   - epithets: list of strings, starts empty

2. TRAITS — THE PERSONALITY ENGINE (three jobs)
   Pool of 12–16: Proud, Cautious, Reckless, Ruthless, Loyal, Vengeful,
   Scholarly, Charming, Stubborn, Ascetic, Greedy, Humble, Righteous, Cold.
   - JOB 1 — weight the yearly action choice (Reckless favors adventure,
     Ascetic favors seclusion, Charming favors socializing).
   - JOB 2 — modify event outcomes (Proud cannot decline a duel; Cautious
     gets a flee bonus; Ruthless kills defeated rivals instead of
     humiliating them — which changes whether the sim generates corpses or
     grudges).
   - JOB 3 — MUTATE UNDER PRESSURE (the single best tool for making
     characters legible over time): a Proud agent crushed in public may
     become Broken or Humble; a Loyal agent betrayed becomes Vengeful;
     surviving a near-death breakthrough can turn anyone Ascetic. The log
     then shows people CHANGING, not just numbers moving.

3. EPITHETS — THE CHEAP MEMORABILITY TRICK
   Any sufficiently dramatic event stamps a descriptive tag: One-Armed,
   Tribulation-Scarred, Oathbreaker, Slayer-of-Wei, Twice-Failed. Zero
   mechanical weight in the toy; enormous readability value. "Han Yu the
   One-Armed reached Core Formation" compresses fifteen simulated years
   into a name, at the cost of a string append.

4. THE YEAR LOOP (three phases; run 200–300 years)

   ACTION PHASE — every agent picks one action, via softmax over trait
   weights plus situational nudges (low qi + rich sect -> cultivate; active
   grudge + Vengeful -> seek the enemy):
   - Cultivate: qi gain x talent; safe.
   - Seclude: more qi, but socially absent — relationships decay, the world
     moves on.
   - Adventure: roll a risk table: death / injury / nothing / resources /
     insight / fateful encounter.
   - Socialize/Scheme: shift standing and relationships; plant or resolve
     grudges.
   - Teach: elders by inclination (not rule); creates master–disciple
     edges, boosts the junior's qi gain.

   EVENT PHASE — collective events:
   - Tournament every 4 years: brackets within realm bands; results move
     standing, forge rivalries, and grant insight to LOSERS WHO SURVIVE.
   - Secret-realm expedition on a random timer: high death, high reward;
     volunteers skew Reckless/Greedy.
   - Sect feud: ignites when accumulated cross-sect grudge intensity passes
     a threshold.
   - Succession crisis: when a sect head dies.

   RESOLUTION PHASE — breakthrough attempts, aging, lifespan checks.

5. CONTESTS — ONE FORMULA
   power = realm x 10 + qi/10 + talent (+ small trait modifiers)
   Keep the tyranny of realms even in the toy: a full realm of difference
   means the lower side can only flee or die. It is what makes the funnel
   and the fear real.

   INSIGHT is granted by adversity, not victory: surviving a loss, a
   near-death, a betrayal, witnessing a higher-realm battle, losing someone
   loved. This single rule reproduces sheltered-genius-plateaus /
   battered-underdog-ascends with no scripting.

6. BREAKTHROUGH AND DEATH
   - At qi 100, attempt breakthrough if insight meets the realm threshold;
     otherwise STALL. Stalling agents increasingly choose Adventure (hunger
     for insight) — a natural feedback loop.
   - Success chance = base + talent + surplus insight - burden.
   - Failure costs qi; from realm 3 up it risks injury or death.
   - Lifespan realm-keyed: mortal ~80 years, each realm adds ~40–60.
   - Death arrives via tribulation, adventure, feud, war, or the quiet
     clock — and the clock matters: a talent-3 Stubborn agent grinding at
     the realm-2 wall until age 130 is exactly the tragic side character
     the sim should produce for free.

7. GENERATIONS FOR FREE
   Every 8 years each sect recruits an intake of fresh agents, age ~14,
   with rolled talent and traits and a couple of pre-seeded relationships
   (siblings, childhood friends, a hometown grudge). The "starting class of
   64" is simply the intake you choose to watch; elders are previous
   intakes that survived; the coming generation arrives on schedule.

8. OUTPUT IS THE PRODUCT
   Every consequential resolution appends a structured log line: year,
   actors, event, outcome, deltas, witnesses. The test: does a raw grep of
   one agent's lines read like a life?

     Y12  Ping Mei (Proud, Reckless, T7) defeats Han Yu in tournament
          final; Han Yu gains grudge(Ping Mei)
     Y15  Secret realm: Ping Mei maimed [epithet: One-Eyed], +12 insight;
          trait Reckless -> Cautious
     Y19  Ping Mei breaks through to Foundation (age 33)
     Y31  Feud, Wei ambush: Ping Mei flees (Cautious); sworn brother
          Luo Gan dies; gains grudge(Wei), +8 insight
     Y44  Ping Mei fails Core tribulation (burden 6); crippled, retires
          to teach
     Y52  Ping Mei's disciple Xie Lan reaches Foundation at 19

   Two derived views:
   - OBITUARY at each death: name, epithets, cause, realm reached, who
     grieved, who celebrated.
   - FINAL ROSTER at year 250.

9. TUNING TARGETS FOR FIRST RUNS
   - FUNNEL SHAPE: per intake of 64, roughly 35–45 to realm 2, a handful
     to realm 4, zero-to-two touching the top over a century. Tune death
     rates and the insight economy until this holds.
   - TALENT VS LUCK: sort final realm by starting talent. Monotone ranking
     = luck too weak, every run predetermined. Uncorrelated = talent is
     decoration. Target: visible correlation with famous exceptions.
   - DISTINGUISHABILITY: pick five random agents, read their logs, check
     each can be described in one sentence. If two lives blur, the fix is
     almost always more trait mutation and more relationship-driven
     events — not more stats.


================================================================================
PART IV — THE PLAYER LAYER (later)
================================================================================

The player character is agent 65: same rules, same funnel, no plot armor.
What changes is resolution, not physics:
- The single NPC resource number unfolds into real inventory, spirit
  stones, pills (with toxicity), treasures, techniques (collectible,
  comprehension-leveled, flawed-manual traps).
- The yearly action unfolds into scene-level play run by the AI DM, which
  shifts from historian to narrator around the player.
- The dao becomes explicit: the player states a principle; play in accord
  generates insight, betrayal plants heart demons that surface at
  tribulations.
- Tribulations become set-piece scenes with real failure states.
- The social ledger becomes visible and interactive: face, oaths, favors,
  karma, sworn brotherhood, grudges with due dates.

Everything around the player keeps running the toy rules — which, by then,
will have been proven able to carry a world on their own.


================================================================================
PART V — BUILD ORDER AND OPEN QUESTIONS
================================================================================

BUILD ORDER
1. Toy sim (Part III). Validate against the three tuning targets by
   reading raw logs. No AI involved.
2. Add refinements: destiny hooks, streaky luck, rumor/witness layer,
   voluntary exits, elder tempo.
3. AI DM as renderer: chronicle generation, obituaries, "state of the
   world" digests from logs.
4. World-generation mode: run 200 years, freeze, use survivors as the
   living setting.
5. Player layer on top of a generated world.

OPEN QUESTIONS (deliberately deferred)
- Narrative gravity: pure simulation will sometimes kill every interesting
  agent and elevate a boring one. Later, does the event system get to
  quietly thumb the scales toward drama (protecting rivalries until they
  resolve, timing reveals), or is the appeal precisely that the world is
  indifferent? Decision: SIM FIRST, CHEAT LATER — revisit only after the
  honest kernel is proven.
- When (if ever) the uniform agent model needs fidelity bands as the world
  scales.
- How much of the dao/heart-demon system NPCs eventually get (currently
  compressed into the burden counter).
